## [0.17.1-alpha] - 2026-10-29

### Bug Fixes
- Restored "I've submitted" with anonymous server verification. All six ratings are required before Google Forms saves a response; confirmed browser identities cannot submit again, and retries reuse the saved receipt. Activation requires the documented Apps Script owner deployment and hosting configuration.
- Campus map buildings now respond to mouse clicks and touch taps while preserving drag controls. The camera also updates when the SVG viewport changes size independently of its container.
