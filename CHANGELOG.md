## [0.21.1] - 11-8-2026

### Added

- Added GNCI-styled 400, 401, 403, 404, and 500 error pages with homepage and contact links, configured through Apache.

### Minor Improvements/Additions

- Restyled error pages with a centered campus pathway layout, faded error codes, a subtle GNC logo watermark, and Noto Serif/Inter typography without a search box.
- Facebook post category editing and filters now include all 13 Announcement and News categories in alphabetical order.
- Updated Announcement and News category choices to the approved lists, sorted alphabetically; existing stored category IDs and posts are preserved.
- Moved the Alumni category option from Announcements to News; Alumni news also appears in the Alumni page's recent events.
- Announcements, News, Facebook Posts, Approvals, and Activity Logs now apply filter selections automatically and search after a short typing pause, removing the Apply and Clear toolbar buttons.
- Approvals now show the newest submissions first by default.

### Bug Fixes

- Filtering for published content no longer prompts for a publication password; publishing actions still require confirmation.
